Building mask review

Local, manual authoring for source-bound binary building masks. Load one original image, paint the visible building pixels, and export a PNG plus a hash-bound JSON record. Nothing is uploaded, and no model is approved or published by this tool.

This tool records an operator's review statement; it does not verify a licence, building identity, segmentation accuracy, camera calibration, or survey measurements. Use only source imagery you are permitted to process. An exported “reviewed” mask is still review evidence—not acceptance or publication approval.
