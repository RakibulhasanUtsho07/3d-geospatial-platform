## Interpretation rules

- **Non-manifold edges — high priority.** `Plinth`, both Rangmahal floors, `Veranda_N`, `Dome_Drum` and `Andarmahal` have non-manifold edges. These may result from intersecting procedural solids but cannot be called harmless without inspecting and classifying the exact joins.
- **Small detached components — high priority.** `Portico_S` and `Portico_N` each report one. The current report does not establish whether these are intended trim pieces or accidental islands; inspect the mesh/render before disposition.
- **Open boundaries and disconnected components — not auto-fail/pass.** Open arches, rails, shutters and separate boxes can be intentional, but warnings remain visible until documented per mesh.
- **Long edges — heuristic.** The global model-diagonal threshold can flag broad planar surfaces. It is a prompt for inspection, not proof that a dimension or building shape is wrong.
- **Context nodes are separate.** Their warnings must not inflate palace-body fidelity scoring, but must not be removed from the raw QA report.
- **Texture state — unresolved.** The model still has zero images, textures and UV accessors. Vertex/material colours do not equal image texture mapping.
