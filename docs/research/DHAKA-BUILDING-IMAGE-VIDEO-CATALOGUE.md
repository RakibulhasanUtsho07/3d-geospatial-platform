**Research date:** 2026-10-10  
**Purpose:** Preserve publicly discoverable visual references and observations for the 3D Geospatial Platform's future building-appearance and procedural-architecture work.  
**Scope note:** This is a curated starter catalogue, not a complete inventory of Dhaka buildings. A source showing a neighbourhood or a property listing is not proof that every visible building is identified or geolocated. No image/video files are copied into this repository; this document stores source links, verified metadata, visual observations and licence cautions.

## 3. Urban residential architecture observations

A Bangladesh real-estate architecture article published in September 2025 describes a broad transition from repetitive rectangular apartment blocks toward more articulated facades. It names Shyamoli, Kalabagan, Malibagh and earlier Bashundhara blocks as examples of the “boxy” era and points to newer design features such as terraced balconies, sun-shading fins, rooftop gardens, greenery, varied textures, daylighting and ventilation. This is contextual reporting—not a substitute for measured building data.

**Patterns worth encoding as procedural parameters (as hypotheses, not universal rules):**
1. **Basic massing:** footprint polygon, floor count, floor-to-floor height, roof/parapet height and any set-back upper levels.
2. **Facade rhythm:** repeated window bays, balcony frequency/depth, openings, grille treatment, vertical shafts and colour/material zones.
3. **Roof and services:** rooftop tanks, stair/lift headhouse, parapets, solar panels or rooftop rooms only when visible in the specific reference.
4. **Street context:** building-to-road gap, narrow lanes, neighbouring wall adjacency, overhead wires and trees as separate context features—not geometry that should be baked into the building mesh.
5. **Heritage-specific parts:** arches, columns, ornamented parapets, courtyard voids and asymmetric wings should be separate components rather than generic apartment decorations.
6. **Climate-sensitive details:** shaded balconies, fins, greenery, ventilation openings and roof gardens may be useful style parameters where images actually show them.

## 4. How this evidence should feed the 3D platform

- **Level 0 — Footprint only:** use the mapped footprint for location and horizontal shape; do not claim facade accuracy.
- **Level 1 — Estimated massing:** combine footprint with a sourced height/floor count where available; otherwise clearly label the height as an estimate.
- **Level 2 — Reference-assisted facade:** use a clearly matched facade image to select repetition, openings, balcony and palette parameters. Keep inferred dimensions marked as estimates.
- **Level 3 — Multi-view reconstruction candidate:** several overlapping views of the same building, captured from distinct useful angles and with consistent provenance, can be assessed for photogrammetry. Merely having three photos/GPS points does not prove a reliable reconstruction is possible.
- **Level 4 — Reviewed model:** compare the reconstructed mesh with source views, flag occluded sides and mismatches, and retain the source/licence record alongside the model.

A single facade image cannot determine rear/side facades, interior floor plans, exact structural dimensions, hidden balconies or materials. GPS proximity alone also cannot prove that a photograph depicts the selected footprint. Models generated from these references must be labelled as estimated unless independently verified.

## 5. Rights, attribution and privacy policy

- Prefer explicitly open-licensed data such as the named Wikimedia Commons files or public KartaView imagery under its stated CC BY-SA terms, while preserving author, source, licence and modification information.
- KartaView's terms state CC BY-SA 4.0 for its street images and require the credit **“© Grab and KartaView Contributors”**. Verify the individual source and current terms when collecting media.
- For Bproperty, Airbnb, news/editorial and architecture-studio pages in this catalogue, no permission to redistribute their imagery was established. Link to them as research sources only.
- Do not infer that a public URL grants permission to copy, bulk-download, train on or republish its media.
- Preserve source attribution in the database using fields such as: `sourceUrl`, `sourceName`, `author`, `capturedAt`, `retrievedAt`, `latitude`, `longitude`, `heading`, `licence`, `attributionText`, `modifications`, `buildingMatchConfidence` and `usageStatus`.
- Use on-demand, bounded collection rather than crawling every image/video host in bulk. Respect the provider's terms, request limits and privacy controls.

## 6. Follow-up research backlog

1. For each KartaView reference returned by the app, record its provider ID, sequence ID, coordinate, heading, capture date and licence attribution; verify that its viewpoint is plausibly about the selected footprint.
2. Build a curated set of named buildings with at least two or more independent facade views where possible, beginning with accessible open-licensed sources.
3. Compare photo references to Overture footprint/height/building-part attributes and store confidence and discrepancies instead of overwriting source geometry.
4. Evaluate a local photogrammetry pipeline (COLMAP or Meshroom/AliceVision) only after a building has enough overlapping, high-quality views.
5. Keep licensed image derivatives and generated meshes in separate asset records with their source/provenance so that one incompatible licence does not silently contaminate the entire dataset.
